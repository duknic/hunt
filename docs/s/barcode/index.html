The lie detector

Every barcode carries a lie detector in its last digit. Someone has been at this one with a marker.

9 3 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0 ?

From the left: multiply the 1st, 3rd, 5th… digits by 1, and the 2nd, 4th, 6th… by 3. Add it all up. The check digit is whatever gets you to the next multiple of ten.
